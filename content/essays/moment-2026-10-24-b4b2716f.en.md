Watching Woody Allen’s films is a joy, because you’re watching a bunch of cynics who scorn everything, deconstruct everything, and possess an almost pathological degree of self-awareness—a bunch of people supremely confident in themselves, constantly taking digs at and mocking one another, convinced they’re living with perfect clarity and ease, never realizing they’ve been inside their own cocoons all along. Rohmer’s films, meanwhile, are about a group of humble people encouraging one another. Everyone feels they’re not doing such a great job of living; they’re willing to admit their confusion, and willing to wait for something language has yet to explain. They know very little about love, yet in truth they live with great clarity, and great happiness.
