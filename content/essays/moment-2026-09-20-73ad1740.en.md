Earth, from you and me receives all things, earth,
I turn back to you, my flesh smelling of holy sacrifice and a sorrow that will not cease until death.
Day and night, I will gaze upward for a long, long time.
I could fill my mouth with bitter soil, amid the sorrow stirred by their voices.
